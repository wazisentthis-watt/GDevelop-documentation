# PART I — THE FIVE RULE-THREADS

Five genuinely different rulesets have been called "CCR" across this project's history. They share a name and a few recurring ideas. They do not share math.

## Thread A — "CCR Game Bible" (PDF)

Chemistry-combat tactics game. Every character = three periodic-table elements: **Crown** (heal/intel) · **Core** (attack/bond chemistry) · **Root** (movement/defense).

- **Triangle Law:** Crown beats Core, Core beats Root, Root beats Crown — the one rule every thread keeps
- **16 legal elements**, each with real EN/color/trait data
- **5-way Core type chart:** Gas > Crystal > Metal > Alkali > Noble > Gas (Heavy is neutral)
- ΔEN-based bond types; real compounds (NaCl, MgO, WF₆...) as damage multipliers
- Native roster: 4 prebuilds — MOTO (Na/F/Fe), MAJI (Si/N/Li), NOVA (Au/Os/Pt), VOID (Xe/Ir/W) — plus custom builds from the 16 elements
- Root sets chess-style movement: Knight/Bishop/Rook/Queen/King
- **Board: 10×8.** Star (★) economy. Simultaneous-reveal turns. A **facing system** (front/side/back changes how the Triangle resolves). Throw mechanic, residue trails, knockback, tile-cracking. Dream Seeds: 2 per piece, respawn not permadeath.
- 12 modes anchored on Arena (1v1/2v2): Free Roam, MansaLife, Dojo, Story Mode, MELLA Mode, Mama Mansa, Records/IRaKodi, Companion (Moto-Maji), Home Building, Vehicle, Guild
- **KoPani in this thread = a simple board pickup.** +5★, +18% HP, respawns in 4 turns. Not a companion, not alive.

# PART IV — THE PARALLEL UNIVERSE SYSTEMS

## BrainBloom 1000 / the Animal Rhythm Atlas / ARK

Stated mission: *"think of 1000 things a person can learn that positively impact their brain, doable on phone or PC with AI help, to make the world more brain healthy."*

A skill only counts if it passes **4 tests**: Action (can you *do* it) · Proof (how shown) · Progress (measurably improvable) · Transfer (helps outside the game).

**118 real skills** across 12 domains (music/rhythm, memory/attention, language, math/logic, science, AI/digital literacy, creativity, emotional/social, life/financial, animals/ecology, body coordination, community/leadership) × **8 forms** (Discover→Practice→Recall→Apply→Create→Cooperate→Teach→Return) = 944, + 56 rare surprise encounters = **1,000 total**. Full itemized list lives in `BrainBloom_118.md`.

**5 mastery levels:** 0 Unopened → 1 First Contact → 2 Assisted Use → 3 **Independent Use (officially "learned")** → 4 Transfer → 5 Teach/Create.

**Surprise encounters** ("shocking Pokémon pop-ups," done on purpose): Wild KoPani Appeared, Memory Mimic, Element Storm, Glitch KoPani, **Golden Rest Encounter** (the challenge is to literally stop and come back later — earns real progress), Mystery Egg, Rival Attuner, Community Rescue.

**The Animal Rhythm Atlas** went through 4 independent full redrafts under different names before being resolved into one blended canon (`ARK_Canon.md`):
- **12 Rhythm Houses**, matching the 12 skill domains 1:1: Pulsekeepers, Strideweavers, Choruskeepers, Grounddrummers, Swarmfields, Sunclockers, Tidekeepers, Routebearers, Display Architects, Decision Strikers, Cyclebearers, Pulse-Lighters
- **Greenhollow Valley** = the starting hub (maps onto Realm Walk's real locations)
- **8 starter paths**, each a lens not a monster: Piga Finch (rhythm), Kumbuka Owl (memory), Uliza Gecko (questions), Jenga Pangolin (building), Kemia Mantis (science), Nuru Cuttle (digital), Sanaa Weaver (creative), Amani Otter (attunement)
- **The Metronome** — the antagonist philosophy, consistent across every draft: believes all rhythm should be standardized. Its weapon is **False Synchronization**. World thesis: *healthy rhythm isn't everybody moving identically — it's different systems finding compatible timing.*
- Core loop: **Notice → Match → Disrupt → Adapt → Join → Return**
- KoPani bond arc: **Five-Petal Accord** (Notice/Match/Adapt/Shared Function/Return)

# PART V — THE THREAD-C MEGA-EXPANSION (the largest body of material in this project)

This is Thread C (Part I) after it stopped being modest. A separate AI conversation expanded it enormously; the output was pasted here across many documents.

**The Rule of Nine:** 9 points distributed across Crown/Core/Root — a temperament map, not just a stat spread. Two KoPani with identical elements can play completely differently depending on the split.

**Five-element KoPani body:** 1 Crown (species identity, up to 118 possible lineages) + 2 Core elements (from a "Core animal") + 2 Root elements (from a "Root animal") — two animal lineages that found survival better together, symbiogenesis as game mythology. Life cycle: **Dream Egg** → **Hatchling** (Core expressed, Root dormant) → **Second Form** (Root physically emerges) → **Adult**.

*Internally unresolved even within this thread:* does the player choose the animal pairing, or does the chemistry generate it? Both models exist in the source material. Proposed (not settled) synthesis: chemistry generates a *range* of compatible forms; environment/ancestry/development pick which one expresses.

**Nakikaru:** the world's environmental intelligence and "Balancer" — explicitly not always on the player's side. In-universe backronym: "Rendering Nuanced Adaptabilities." Central unresolved tension, stated as a real philosophical conflict in the material itself: does correcting imbalance protect freedom, or does it become quiet control?

**The 8 Philosophy Continents**, each a signature animal-pair hybrid: Lotus (Buddha, Crane+Turtle) · Ubuntu Savannah (Lion+Elephant) · Socratic Plateau (Owl+Fox) · Tao Forest (Panda+Snake) · Da Vinci Foundry (Falcon+Mantis) · Nietzsche Ridge (Jaguar+Hawk — the source material itself flags this one as needing careful framing so it doesn't glorify domination) · Darwin Isles (Falcon+Shark/Iguana) · Octoverse Archipelago (Octopus+Dolphin — no single-philosopher anchor; its law is that intelligence doesn't need to live in one center).

**Elemental Soldering:** two KoPani sharing a Crown lineage can consciously reconfigure their Core/Root partnership. What's released becomes **Free Ground DNA**, enters the mycelial network as spores, and can recombine into wild **Mushroom Eggs** — nothing is permanently wasted, and no lineage is ever collection filler.

**TNA (Traversal Neural Abilities):** the neurological layer determining whether a body can actually use anatomy it has. Connects to a real, separate biomechanics/movement-coaching project called **Mi'Wendo** (Sense→Interpret→Coach→Reward→Protect; Crown=intent, Core=mechanics, Root=safety as real movement-scoring lenses).

**Binary Bark, reimagined:** a robot containing an internal insect civilization (**IIC** — Inspectoral Insect City) with real districts (Sensor/Signal/Hardware/Armor/Cooling/Composition), able to fabricate new body parts mid-battle from consumed matter ("Available Assembly Potential"). This version has nothing to do with the simple 4-ability story companion of the same name in the shipped v14 file — same name, unrelated character.

**The player's own pre-built MELLA** ("the Element Architect / Balancer"): Crown Gold · Core Gallium+Iridium · Root Silicon+Selenium · Catalyst Bismuth · Core-animal Peregrine Falcon · Root-animal Mimic Octopus.

**The 25 Reality Overwrites** ("Singularity"/"Black-Sun" mode): a deliberately-broken chaos layer — half-life decay, phase-state inversion, Nakikaru turning dictatorial, hardware failure as gameplay. **Canon ruling stated in the source material itself:** this is a contained anomaly mode layered on top of the normal rules, never the default — and real hardware must always fail safely no matter what the fiction shows.

### What five independent expert-review documents actually agreed on

Five separate ~27-specialist simulated review passes, written independently, converged on the same fixes without coordinating:

1. Label every rule: real science / plausible extrapolation / game abstraction / deliberate override
2. Every animal (real or KoPani) needs individual variation — no fixed universal trait
3. Accessibility and consent/welfare designed in from day one
4. Any Balancer AI must be auditable — never secretly manipulating outcomes
5. Real hardware safety stays completely walled off from fictional failure states
6. **Ship one small vertical slice before the full 118** — said independently, almost verbatim, by all five. `CCR_Octoverse.html` was built specifically because of this convergence.

# PART VI-A — THE RING MALL

A new location, built directly out of the roster in Part VI: a shopping hub structured as a series of concentric rings — visually like rings around a planet, not a flat building. Different rings can carry different WIR franchises (AMU/Octo-Matrix, Planet Nem, Constellation Saga, and the rest), so walking inward or outward through the Mall is walking between shows.

**What it sells:** two kinds of things, kept distinct on purpose —
1. **In-game tools** — items that help you play right now
2. **Build-shaping items** — things that help you design your character's *overall* game, not just the next fight

**The economy it runs on, stated sharply:** Mansa Money should be easy to get. Not a grind — "not a thing where you gotta bust your balls to get money." But it's not free either: you earn it by doing things that make sense *for your specific character*, not generic tasks. The full loop:

> Do something that fits your character → it pays you → spend it at the Mall on something that fits your character → that lets you do *more* things that fit your character → which pays you more.

A self-reinforcing, personalized economy, not a flat grind-and-spend one. This sharpens the Mansa Money principle already established elsewhere in this document (Part III/VI) rather than replacing it — the market-job pattern already in Realm Walk is the small-scale version of exactly this same loop.

# PART VII — DESIGN PHILOSOPHY (stated directly, never inferred)

- Conceived CCR around age 6–10, sparring with cousins — fighting stopped being fun once he was clearly stronger, and he wanted a way to slow combat down while keeping it real. Straight chess felt boring because a piece just loses on contact, no strategy left once you reach it. Football (blocking, open paths) seeded Maze Run — "combat as a moving maze."
- Explicit anti-engagement-capture ethic: the game should hold attention because being here is genuinely good for the player, not through attention-capture engineering — stated in direct contrast to how GTA's expansion into other activities reads as being about not losing the player to something else.
- The fitness/Mansa Money honor-system claim is deliberate, not a flaw — the honor system itself is what builds the player's self-accountability.
- KoPani exist to teach real chemistry experientially — "the living version of the elements."
- Wants CCR run through an LLM game master specifically because per-piece movement legality and live chemistry explanation only work with something tracking both simultaneously.
