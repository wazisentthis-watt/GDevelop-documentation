# ARK: Ani-Mdundo Attunement
### The Living Groove — canon cut

*Ani = animal life. Mdundo = rhythm. Attunement = a regulated relationship, not a command.*

This is the one version. Where the drafts disagreed, this is the call — and why.

---

## 1. The law everything else sits on

**Biological Precedence.** Before anything gets called a power, name which living system discovered the principle first. Humans didn't invent elastic energy return, seismic communication, or distributed coordination — a cheetah's spine, an elephant's feet, and a school of fish had all three long before we had words for them. An element is never a personality. Iron isn't "strength," Copper isn't "electricity." The chain is always:

**Element → bond → molecule → structure → organ → behavior → ecological function → Attunement ability**

The animal is the curriculum. The fantasy doesn't replace the biology — it makes the biology impossible to ignore.

## 2. What a KoPani actually is

Three states, and most animals never leave the first one:

- **Natural animal** — just alive, doing what it does. Most of the world.
- **Ani-Elemental** — an individual whose real rhythm has become unusually legible (injury recovered from, a rare habitat, long partnership with something else). Still fully autonomous.
- **KoPani** — an Ani-Elemental in an active, renewable Accord with a person. Not owned. Not captured. Chosen, continuously.

The game never says *"creature captured."* It says *"a living route opened."* You're not proving you can overpower life — you're proving you're trustworthy enough for the relationship to keep going.

## 3. The loop, everywhere

**Notice → Match → Disrupt → Adapt → Join → Return**

- **Notice** — you observe the real rhythm before anyone explains it to you
- **Match** — you reproduce enough of it to show you heard it (this is the piano — see §6)
- **Disrupt** — something real interferes: noise, artificial light, fatigue, a crowded signal
- **Adapt** — you change strategy, not volume
- **Join** — a shared rhythm forms that belongs to neither of you alone
- **Return** — you close it safely and leave something restored, not just defeated

This loop is the whole game. Combat runs on it. Bonding runs on it. Learning a real-world skill runs on it. One engine, three catalogs — see §8.

## 4. The twelve houses

Every one of the 118 animals belongs to a house — not a type, a *way of carrying time*.

| House | Carries time through | Teaches |
|---|---|---|
| Pulsekeepers | breath, heartbeat, oxygen | pacing, recovery, lowering demand |
| Strideweavers | gait, elastic return, body waves | switching pattern instead of forcing speed |
| Choruskeepers | duets, turn-taking, song | listening as part of expression |
| Grounddrummers | vibration through soil, web, wall | reading a signal through matter |
| Swarmfields | neighbor-to-neighbor coordination | order without one commander |
| Sunclockers | circadian timing, sleep | acting at the right hour, not just fast |
| Tidekeepers | lunar and tidal windows | some doors only open on schedule |
| Routebearers | migration, generational travel | carrying a journey you won't finish alone |
| Display Architects | courtship, constructed space | practice and presentation |
| Decision Strikers | predictive timing, commitment | one correct action beats ten fast ones |
| Cyclebearers | molt, dormancy, emergence | growth includes real vulnerability |
| Pulse-Lighters | electric and light signaling | protecting a channel, not just amplifying it |

Twelve houses, twelve regions — the map is the skill tree.

## 5. The world map

**Greenhollow Valley** is home. It's not one of the twelve — it's the hub, and it's already half-built: your Starter Home, the Groove Garden, Kio-Mane Dojo, Arena Commons, the KoPani Grove, the small market, the riverwalk. Every path loops back here. This is Realm Walk, already shipped.

From the Valley, the twelve houses become twelve reachable regions — Pulse Depths, Stride Savannas, Chorus Canopy, Groundsong Range, Swarmfield Cities, Sunclock Desert, Tide Calendar Coast, Routebearer Skies, Display Gardens, Decision Wilds, Cycle Caverns, Pulse-Light Reefs. Each one is built from a different physical relationship with time, not a different color palette.

## 6. The piano is not a minigame bolted on — it's how Notice and Match actually happen

You don't select "Match" from a menu. You hear the KoPani's phrase — real notes, real timing, on a keyboard played like a piano — and you answer it. This part is **already built and tested**: 8 KoPani in Realm Walk each have a real 3-note phrase, played with real Web Audio tones and synced key-lighting, echoed back on-screen or on A-S-D-F-G-H-J-K. A wrong note never loses the KoPani — it just asks you to listen again.

That's Orbit Bond, the first tier. The full ladder, not yet built:

**Orbit Bond** (3-note Echo, live) → **Resonance Bond** (4-6 notes) → **Direct Bond** (real call-and-response) → **Garden Bond** (two-hand, chords) → **Groove Bond** (you and the KoPani hold separate parts at once) → **Return Bond** (it can change the phrase mid-fight and you adapt live)

## 7. The Five-Petal Accord

This is the relationship arc the piano sits inside:

**Notice** (you correctly read the rhythm) → **Match** (you answer it) → **Adapt** (you hold up when it's disrupted) → **Shared Function** (you do something real together — a rescue, a repair, not just a fight) → **Return** (the animal, and its world, get something back)

A five-petal KoPani isn't stronger. It's more deeply *itself*, and still fully able to leave.

## 8. One engine, three catalogs

This is the piece that was scattered across six documents and is actually one idea:

**BrainBloom** (118 real human skills), **the Animal Rhythm Atlas** (118 real animals), and **WishBorn** (open-ended emotional DreamSeeds — Courage, Patience, Focus, Boundary, Curiosity...) are not three separate systems. They're three different *inputs* into the same eight-stage engine:

**Discover → Practice → Recall → Apply → Create → Cooperate → Teach → Return**

A skill runs through it and becomes independently learnable. An animal runs through it and becomes a KoPani relationship. A DreamSeed runs through it and becomes five Leaves, then a Wish Orb, then a RootRealizer. Same shape, three different kinds of growth. WishBorn is what happens when you point the engine at *you* instead of at a skill or an animal.

**Where it lands in the systems that already exist:** a DreamSeed picks its KoPani by rhythm, not by element — Courage suits something built on predictive timing, Patience suits something built on oxygen budgeting. In CCR specifically, a Seed changes your SP rhythm, a RootRealizer changes the board rather than fighting for you, and a Wish Orb decides which MELLA variant you get. In [[ai-and-us]], every Leaf becomes a star — literally the same eight stages, plotted as a constellation instead of a garden.

The starter doesn't lock you into one kind of intelligence. It just decides which door you walk through first.

## 10. Your Garden is the readable version of your growth

Not a meter. A place you can walk through and *see*. Every plant is something you've practiced, moving through **Seed → Sprout → Stem → Bloom → Branch → Fruit → Grove → Return Root** as it deepens. Walk your own Garden and you can see — without being told — whether your music plants are thriving while your practical-life corner sits empty. It doesn't shame you. It just shows you where your attention has actually gone.

## 11. The antagonist, because a world needs one true disagreement

**The Metronome** believes irregularity is inefficient — every child learning at the same speed, every KoPani playing the same phrase, every rhythm synchronized for "harmony." Its signature weapon is **False Synchronization**: technology that copies the outer shape of a real bond — a flash, a pulse, a call — without the consent, context, or repair underneath it.

The whole world's answer to it, stated once, plainly: *healthy rhythm isn't everybody moving identically — it's different systems finding compatible timing.* That line is the actual spine of the setting. Everything else is downstream of it.

## 12. What's real right now vs. what's still just this document

**Built and tested:** Greenhollow Valley (Realm Walk — the walkable map, 9 real locations), the sound-trail discovery system, Orbit Bond piano bonding for 8 KoPani, the Garden→Groove→KoPani loop, the Arena Bridge into real CCR combat, 24 KoPani tied to real elements with real chemistry, the full 129-fighter combat engine.

**Real vision, not yet code:** everything past Orbit Bond on the piano ladder, WishBorn's Seeds/Leaves/Orbs/RootRealizers, the twelve regions as walkable spaces, the eight starter paths, the Garden's eight growth stages, the Metronome as an actual storyline, and BrainBloom's Discovery Run.

Nothing in §12's second list contradicts anything already shipped. It's all built to sit directly on top of it.
